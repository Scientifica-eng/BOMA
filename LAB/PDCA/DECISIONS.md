# PDCA Documentation Decisions

## D-001 — One significant experiment, one document

**Status:** Provisional / under test  
**Cycle:** `PDCA-001`

### Decision

Each significant experiment in the PDCA laboratory is documented as an independent document.

The index is navigation only; it is not the authoritative experimental record.

### Rationale

Independent experiment documents preserve:

- the question being tested;
- the initial hypothesis;
- the method or conceptual setup;
- observed results;
- failures and anomalies;
- corrections;
- learning;
- limitations;
- open questions;
- evidence and provenance;
- epistemic status.

This prevents independent experiments from being merged merely because they concern the same subject.

### Historical preservation

During migration, the original experimental records are not silently deleted or rewritten. A migrated document identifies its source record.

A later correction should preserve the earlier conclusion and record the correction with explicit provenance.

### Scope

This is a **laboratory documentation decision**, not a BOMA requirement.

It is itself subject to PDCA review. If the structure proves cumbersome or misleading, it may be revised with a new decision record rather than silently changing history.
