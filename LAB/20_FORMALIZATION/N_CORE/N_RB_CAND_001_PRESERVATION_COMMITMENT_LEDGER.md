# 11. Learning extracted

1. A faithful formalization must preserve not only mathematical results but **which side of the formalization boundary produced them**.
2. Global carrier formation is the largest new commitment in R-B and must never be recoded as a derived TCT result.
3. Backend datatypes can be structurally isomorphic to TCT presentation syntax without becoming the canonical pre-boundary ontology.
4. The same structural property may be `DERIVED` twice—once pre-formally/constructionally and once inside the formal regime—with different proof provenance.
5. A commitment ledger should be re-audited after machine checking because proof failures can expose hidden assumptions.
