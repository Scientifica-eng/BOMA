# PDSA-Z-001 — Integer Acceptance Target

**Status:** CLOSED — `BOMA-Z-ACCEPT-001 v1.0` ADOPTED

## Study
The integer target must be representation-neutral. In particular, quotient pairs must not be assumed merely because they are standard, and signed normal forms must not be assumed merely because they are computationally convenient.

## Act
Run signed-normal-form and difference-pair routes in parallel. Require representation convergence before opening the canonical-representation Decision Point.
