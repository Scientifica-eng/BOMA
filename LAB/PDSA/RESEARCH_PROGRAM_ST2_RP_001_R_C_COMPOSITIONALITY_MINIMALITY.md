## 1. Research objective

Stage Two has already established independent real and complex alternatives and
measured two distinct R→C dependency surfaces. This program asks the next
mature questions:

```text
1. Can the selected C meaning be rebuilt completely over the independent
   Cauchy real producer without Dedekind implementation transport?
2. Do the independently verified R and C route choices compose, rather than
   merely work in isolation?
3. Is real total order a mathematical requirement of the selected quadratic
   field construction, or can the current use of orderTotal be replaced by a
   strictly weaker algebraic nondegeneracy condition?
4. If such a weaker condition is sufficient, can the C construction be
   factored over a generic scalar field K, revealing K[i] rather than R-specific
   ancestry as the underlying structure?
```

The program deliberately progresses from producer substitution, to route
composition, to dependency weakening, to structural generalization.
