## 1. Purpose

Convert verified lessons from `ST2-EXP-001`, `ST2-EXP-002`, and `ST2-EXP-003`
from experiment-only presentation into permanent architectural knowledge in the
BOMA Construction DAG.

This Act does **not** erase the Learning Graph. It adds a one-way promotion of
verified knowledge:

```text
successful experiment evidence
        ↓
Study / Act classification
        ↓
permanent Construction-DAG knowledge
```

without the invalid implication:

```text
permanent DAG visibility
        ≠
selected route
        ≠
accepted export
```

## 2. Governing integration rule

A successful experiment may change the core architecture in one or more of the
following ways when explicitly owner-authorized:

1. refine an existing dependency contract with a smaller verified interface;
2. preserve a verified non-selected route as a permanent alternative branch;
3. preserve a verified reconvergence result as a permanent Junction;
4. expose a representation-independent invariant learned across producers;
5. record a new reopening condition or genericity requirement for future work.

Promotion of **knowledge** is distinct from promotion of a **producer**.
Changing `SELECTS`, replacing an accepted Block/export, or adding a new accepted
Claim still requires its own explicit acceptance/Decision action and evidence.
