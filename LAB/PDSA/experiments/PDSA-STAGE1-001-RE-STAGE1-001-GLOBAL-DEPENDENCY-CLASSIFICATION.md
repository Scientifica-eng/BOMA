## What the baseline actually proves

- The six declared number-domain interfaces are independently formalized and
  accepted within an explicit trusted metatheory; they are not derived from
  mathematical nothingness.
- Distinct construction histories can converge without becoming the same
  historical path: natural arithmetic routes and integer signed/pair routes
  provide actual reconvergence evidence.
- Quotient infrastructure is selected for accepted Q and selected Dedekind R;
  it is not thereby necessary to every rational, real, or complex construction.
- Real completeness, density, and Archimedean behavior are required by the
  selected accepted R contract; they are not direct mathematical requirements
  of the selected C quadratic-extension contract.
- The concrete final C Lean closure nevertheless inherits those R producers
  because it consumes a whole concrete R integration certificate. Packaging
  dependence must be measured rather than erased or confused with necessity.
- Accepted R has five localized source-level classical sites; selected C adds
  no new C-local site but really consumes `orderTotal` from the current real
  producer and inherits other classical paths via concrete upstream packaging.
- Accepted C exposes universal quadratic comparison relations; actual function
  data requires explicitly supplied coordinate extractors. `C-ROUTE-Q` and
  Cauchy remain retained candidates, not completed reconverged domains.
- No stage establishes algebraic closure of C, absence of metatheory, uniqueness
  of all foundational choices, global intuitionistic purity, or a proof of the
  consistency of all mathematical foundations.
