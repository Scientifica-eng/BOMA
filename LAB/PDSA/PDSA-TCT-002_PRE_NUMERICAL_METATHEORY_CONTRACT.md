# PLAN

## ResearchQuestion

What meta-level resources may BOMA use to describe and verify a pre-numerical TCT construction without importing those same resources as hidden object-level mathematical structure?

## Subquestions

1. What does `finite` mean before numerical cardinality is constructed?
2. May TCT use grammar and recursion?
3. May structural induction be used as a proof technique?
4. May external natural numbers appear in documents and tooling?
5. May sets/types/lists in a proof assistant represent TCT syntax?
6. What separates meta-level identity from object-level structural equivalence?
7. How should ellipses and phrases such as “all generated configurations” be interpreted before a global carrier exists?
8. Which logical principles must be declared if materially used?

## Risk model

The principal risk inherited from CAL-001 is not merely the appearance of numerical vocabulary. It is **cross-boundary export**:

```text
external descriptive/proof resource
        ↓ silently imported
object-level mathematical commitment
```

The cycle therefore seeks a firewall rather than an impossible claim of reasoning without a metatheory.

## Acceptance criteria

A contract is acceptable if:

- it makes pre-numerical an object-level claim rather than a metatheory-free claim;
- it gives an auditable meaning to meta-level finiteness and recursion;
- it prevents meta-level arithmetic from proving object-level numerical structure by camouflage;
- it distinguishes syntactic identity from structural equivalence;
- it does not itself decide `≈` or close `TCT-J-001`;
- it preserves the ability to use external proof/model/countermodel tools transparently.

# Observations

## O1 — “Pre-numerical” is sustainable only with a boundary distinction

Trying to remove all external number, syntax, recursion, data structures, or proof machinery would make the project impossible to document and would confuse research infrastructure with mathematical content.

The scientifically defensible claim is therefore object-level:

> numerical concepts are not imported as premises of the constructed domain before an explicit construction/formalization boundary.

## O2 — Meta-level natural numbers are already unavoidable as infrastructure

Dates, commit IDs, line numbers, cycle numbers, file counts, and proof-assistant representations already use ordinary external mathematics.

This is harmless **only if it remains external**.

## O3 — The word “finite” requires qualification, not prohibition

The historical risk arose when “finite” was used to imply count/depth inside the developing object structure.

For research purposes, an explicitly written terminating derivation can be described externally as finite without thereby constructing finite cardinality internally.

## O4 — Recursive grammar does not imply a completed global carrier

A grammar can license any supplied finite derivation without asserting the existence of a completed set/type of all possible derivations as an object inside TCT.

This repeats and sharpens the Closure Core distinction between generative availability and global totality.

## O5 — Equality notation is a pending architectural issue

TCT-001 writes empty behavior using:

```text
ε ⊙ A = A
A ⊙ ε = A
```

while associativity is targeted only up to:

```text
(A ⊙ B) ⊙ C ≈ A ⊙ (B ⊙ C)
```

This creates a question for `PDSA-TCT-003`:

- Is `=` intended as definitional/syntactic equality?
- Is it strict object-level identity?
- Should empty behavior instead be expressed via `≈`?
- What structure must `≈` preserve relative to `⊙` and `ε`?

The metatheory contract must not answer this by fiat.

## O6 — “one elementary block” must not be read as a cardinality theorem

Historical TCT text sometimes says “one elementary block U.” Under the current contract this is read as **a designated elementary block role/symbol**, not as an internally derived numerical `1`.

Canonical wording should prefer `an elementary block U` where ambiguity matters.

# STUDY

## Central learning

The correct anti-circularity mechanism is not vocabulary censorship. It is a **typed epistemic boundary** between what belongs to the research metalanguage and what belongs to the mathematical object being constructed.

The project may externally know and use natural numbers while still investigating whether a given object-level structure can be built without assuming them as object-level premises.

This distinction is analogous to using a programming language to implement a formal system without treating every host-language feature as an axiom of the implemented theory.

## Why this improves on the historical path

The earlier calibration identified suspicious words individually (`one`, `finite`, `depth`). The new contract generalizes the lesson:

> The risk is not the word itself; the risk is **untracked export across the meta/object boundary**.

This makes the rule reusable for later concepts such as sets, quotients, equivalence classes, induction, completion, order, and proof-assistant data types.

## Does the contract prove anything mathematical?

No.

It is a declared methodological/metatheoretic contract governing permissible reasoning and classification.

It does not prove:

- existence of all finite configurations as a global object;
- associativity;
- empty identity laws at a chosen identity level;
- terminal cancellation;
- canonical decomposition;
- natural numbers.
