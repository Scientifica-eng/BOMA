# Brick B-002 — Referable Constructional Result

**Status:** Provisional
**Type:** Chosen constructional extension
**Predecessor:** `B-001`
**Experiment:** `EXP-016`

## Definition

Let `r` denote the **referable constructional result** produced by the distinction-making act `δ` of `B-001`.

At this stage, `r` means only:

> the result-role that the construction permits us to refer to after `δ` has been performed.

It is not yet declared to be:

- an independently existing object;
- a number;
- a set;
- a member of a set;
- equal to or different from another object;
- persistent under repetition.

## Why this Brick was chosen

`B-001` gives us a distinction-making act but no referable outcome. To continue an actual brick-by-brick construction, we need some minimal way to refer to what the construction has produced.

This is an explicit choice, not a deduction claimed to be forced by `B-001`.

## Current commitments

1. The construction may designate a result-role `r`.
2. Reference to `r` does not yet imply a general theory of objects.
3. No equality relation is introduced.
4. No numerical multiplicity is introduced.
5. No set or membership relation is introduced.
6. Any stronger interpretation requires a later Brick or explicit revision.

## Current structure

```text
S-001
  │
  ▼
 δ  ─────►  r
 B-001      B-002
```

## What remains open

We must test whether `r` can participate in another construction. In particular, we must avoid silently treating `r` as an ordinary object merely because we have given it a symbol.

## Epistemic status

**Provisional / experimental.**
