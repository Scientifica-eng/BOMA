# Brick B-010 — Local Successor-Like Structure

**Status:** Provisional
**Type:** Structural consequence of the chosen construction
**Previous Brick:** `B-009`
**Experiment:** `EXP-031`

## Definition

Within the constructed finite-stage history, each completed stage has one designated immediate extension, and each non-initial stage has one recorded immediate predecessor.

```text
s₀ → s₁ → s₂ → s₃ → ...
```

## Why this Brick was created

`B-009` made stages referable. `EXP-031` showed that the chosen extension rule supplies a locally unique next stage and that each non-initial stage records its immediate predecessor.

This is a structural result relative to the construction history, not an imported definition of the natural-number successor function.

## Current commitments

1. Each constructed stage has one designated immediate extension under `E`.
2. Each non-initial constructed stage has one recorded immediate predecessor.
3. The relation is local to the constructional history.
4. The relation is called successor-like, not yet the formal successor function of arithmetic.
5. No completed infinite domain is assumed.
6. No induction principle is assumed.
7. No numeral interpretation is assumed.

## What the Brick gives us

A chain structure on finite constructional stages.

The chain is generated by the extension rule rather than postulated as an independently existing set of numbers.

## What remains open

The initial stage must now be analyzed. We need to determine what structurally characterizes it and whether that characterization can support a zero-like starting object without simply assigning the symbol `0` by fiat.

## Epistemic status

**Provisional structural result.**
