# Brick B-001 — Distinction Marker

**Status:** Provisional
**Type:** Chosen constructional primitive
**Starting point:** `S-001`
**Experiment:** `EXP-015`

## Definition

We introduce `δ` as a primitive **distinction-making act**.

`δ` is not, at this stage, declared to be:

- an object;
- a number;
- a set;
- a proposition;
- a binary relation;
- an equality relation.

Its sole intended role is to make a distinction available within the construction.

## Why this Brick was chosen

This is an explicit constructional choice. It is **not derived** from `S-001` and is not claimed to be the unique possible starting Brick.

The choice is motivated by the project requirement to construct a pre-numerical basis while avoiding the immediate introduction of already formed objects and numerical multiplicity.

## Current commitments

1. A distinction-making act is admitted as a primitive.
2. No entities are thereby assumed to exist.
3. No multiplicity is thereby assumed.
4. No equality is thereby assumed.
5. The Brick must not be interpreted more strongly than its declared role.
6. Future Bricks must not contradict these commitments unless a later experiment explicitly revises them.

## What the Brick gives us

At present, only a constructional possibility of distinction:

```text
S-001
  │
  ▼
  δ
```

It does **not** yet give us a distinguishable object.

## What remains open

The next construction must determine whether `δ` can yield a referable result, role, or other stable constructional entity. If an additional primitive is required, that primitive must be declared explicitly rather than hidden in notation.

## Epistemic status

**Provisional / experimental.**

This Brick is part of an ongoing construction experiment. It is not presented as a theorem, canonical foundation, or final philosophical claim.
