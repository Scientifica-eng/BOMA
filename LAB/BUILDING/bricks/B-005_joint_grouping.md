# Brick B-005 — Joint Grouping of Referable Tokens

**Status:** Provisional
**Type:** Chosen constructional operation
**Previous Brick:** `B-004`
**Experiment:** `EXP-022`

## Definition

We introduce `G` as an operation that can take two provenance-distinguished referable tokens jointly as input:

```text
G(r₁,r₂) → g
```

The result `g` is a constructional grouping, not yet identified as a set, pair, number, or other familiar mathematical object.

## Why this Brick was chosen

`B-004` provides controlled pre-numerical multiplicity: two referable tokens are available. The next question is whether the construction can treat them together without importing set theory or numerical cardinality.

`G` is therefore an explicit experimental choice.

## Current commitments

1. Two referable tokens can be jointly selected by one construction.
2. The resulting construction `g` has the two tokens as its current input.
3. No order relation is assumed.
4. No set membership relation is assumed.
5. No cardinality is assigned.
6. `g` is not identified with the number two.
7. Whether order matters remains open.

## What the Brick gives us

```text
r₁   r₂
 \   /
   G
   │
   g
```

This gives us a first candidate for a finite grouping operation while remaining explicitly pre-numerical.

## What remains open

We must test whether reversing the inputs produces the same construction, a different construction, or an unresolved case.

## Epistemic status

**Provisional / chosen construction.**
