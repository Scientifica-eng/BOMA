# EXP-XXX — Experiment Record

**Brick / context:** `B-XXX`  
**Status:** Provisional

## 1. Intention / Goal

What are we trying to build, test, or determine?

## 2. Experiment

What did we actually do?

## 3. Result

What happened? Record success, failure, unexpected behavior, or unresolved outcome.

## 4. Decision

What do we decide because of the result?

## 5. Epistemic Status

`Provisional` / `Accepted` / `Rejected` / `Open`

## 6. Next Cycle

What will the next experiment attempt?

---

### Rules

- One record per experiment.
- Keep it concise; preserve only information needed to reconstruct the reasoning.
- Record failures; do not silently replace them with corrected versions.
- Separate **result** from **decision**: a failed formulation does not necessarily mean the underlying idea is rejected.
- Do not promote a provisional observation into a BOMA rule without further evidence.
- If an experiment produces a major architectural insight, create a separate detailed document and reference it here.
