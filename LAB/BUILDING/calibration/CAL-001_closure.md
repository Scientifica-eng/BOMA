# CAL-001 — Cycle Closure

**Status:** Closed  
**Scope:** B-001–B-014 and calibration experiments CAL-EXP-001–006

## 1. Intention / Goal

Determine the smallest methodologically defensible starting point after auditing the first construction path for hidden numerical commitments, and decide how the repaired path should proceed.

## 2. Consolidated Findings

### 2.1 Starting condition

The project does not claim to begin from absolute nothingness.

The minimal condition identified so far is:

```text
perceptible/registerable difference
        ↓
possibility of distinction
        ↓
distinction δ
```

The first item is treated as a **condition of possibility**, not as a Brick. It is not assigned a numerical structure and is not presented as an absolute metaphysical primitive.

### 2.2 First constructive act

`δ` remains the first explicit constructive operation.

Its role is not to create difference from nothing. It acts when a difference can be registered and produces a constructional distinction.

### 2.3 B-001–B-004

These remain usable, subject to the refined interpretation above.

- `B-001`: distinction — primitive constructive choice.
- `B-002`: referability — derived persistence of a distinguished result.
- `B-003`: reuse — operational persistence/reapplication.
- `B-004`: provenance — structural record of constructional origin.

### 2.4 B-005–B-008

`B-005` (`G`) is retained as an exploratory branch, not a necessary step in the current path.

`B-007` exposes a genuine hidden-numerical risk in the phrase "exactly one". The repaired interpretation treats the uniqueness of the designated new output as part of the chosen extension protocol, not as a theorem derived from a number-free base.

### 2.5 B-013–B-014

The numerical-depth formulation is superseded.

The preferred replacement is structural equivalence of construction histories and preservation of that equivalence under uniform extension.

```text
history H₁ ≃ H₂
        ↓ E       ↓ E
      E(H₁) ≃ E(H₂)
```

No numerical depth is required for this formulation.

### 2.6 Identification of N

Construction and identification are separated.

The eventual natural-number claim must have the form:

```text
chosen primitives
      ↓
constructed structure C
      ↓
independent structural analysis
      ↓
proof of a characterization of N
      ↓
C ≅ N
```

A characterization of the natural numbers may be used as an identification test, but not as a hidden recipe for constructing `C`.

## 3. Decision

### Decision A — No new Brick for the precondition

The "perceptible/registerable difference" will **not** receive a Brick number at this stage.

Reason: it is currently functioning as a condition of possibility for the construction, not as a constructed object produced by a Brick operation. Giving it a Brick number would risk conflating the precondition of the system with an object inside the system.

It will remain explicitly documented in calibration and foundation/context records.

### Decision B — B-001 remains the first Brick

`B-001` remains the first numbered Brick because it is the first explicit constructive act in the repaired path: distinction `δ`.

This also resolves the earlier question of why numbering should begin at `B-001`: the numbering indexes **constructed Bricks**, not preconditions of construction.

### Decision C — Historical Bricks are preserved

The original formulations of `B-013` and `B-014` are not erased. They remain historical experimental records. Their numerical formulations are marked superseded for the repaired path.

### Decision D — No automatic continuation to B-015

The next Brick must not simply continue the old numbering sequence. We must first construct the repaired version of the earliest path and verify that every Brick satisfies the calibration rules.

## 4. New Working Principle

The following rule is adopted for future construction:

> **Every commitment must be classified as a declared primitive, a derived consequence, a linguistic convenience, or an unresolved assumption. No numerical concept may enter by linguistic camouflage.**

A second rule follows:

> **A condition of possibility is not automatically an object of the constructed domain.**

This distinction is now part of the project's methodology.

The calibration did not prove that the project will reach the natural numbers.

It did establish a cleaner starting architecture:

## 6. Open Questions Carried Forward

1. Can the refined B-001 be formalized without importing identity, equality, set membership, or number?
2. Can referability in B-002 be defined without silently importing identity?
3. Can provenance in B-004 become a structural relation rather than a hidden label system?
4. Can the extension protocol be defined without numerical uniqueness language?
5. Can structural equivalence of histories be constructed without importing a ready-made theory of finite sequences?
6. What is the minimal internal mechanism corresponding to induction/closure before the natural numbers are identified?

## 7. Final Decision

**CAL-001 CLOSED.**

The repaired construction path begins again at `B-001`, with the precondition of perceptible/registerable difference documented as context rather than as a Brick.

The next cycle should therefore be a **reconstruction cycle**, not a continuation cycle.
