# CAL-EXP-001 — Audit of B-001 through B-004

**Cycle:** `CAL-001`  
**Scope:** `B-001`–`B-004`  
**Status:** Provisional audit

## 1. Intention / Goal

Audit the earliest constructional commitments and determine whether distinction, referability, reuse, multiplicity, and provenance already presuppose numerical structure.

## 2. Experiment

For each early Brick, separate the operational act from the language used to describe it.

### B-001 — Distinction

The act of distinguishing is treated as a primitive choice. It does not require an already constructed number system. However, it does presuppose that there is some content or occurrence to which the act applies.

**Classification:** `P/U`.

This means the project is not beginning from absolute nothingness. It begins from a minimal chosen act of distinction applied to some encountered content.

### B-002 — Referability

A distinguished result is made available for later reference.

Reference is not itself numerical. It requires persistence of an identified result, but no counting structure.

**Classification:** `D` relative to the chosen distinction act, with the exact semantics of identity/reference remaining `U`.

### B-003 — Reuse

The previously referable result can participate again in a later operation.

This is operational persistence, not numerical repetition.

**Classification:** `D`.

### B-004 — Provenance

Two referable results may be distinguished by their constructional origin/history.

This does not require cardinal arithmetic. It introduces a structural record of origin.

**Classification:** `D/P`, depending on whether provenance recording is treated as an explicit feature of the construction protocol or derived from the history itself.

## 3. Result

No direct numerical commitment is required in B-001 through B-004 if the following interpretation is maintained:

```text
initial distinction = declared primitive choice
reference = persistence of a distinguished result
reuse = persistence across operations
provenance = structural record of origin
```

The audit nevertheless exposes an important boundary condition:

> The project must not describe the initial distinction as arising from "zero", "one", a set, a count, or a completed logical universe.

The project is therefore better described as beginning from a **minimal constructive act**, not from absolute ontological nothingness.

## 4. Decision

Retain B-001 through B-004 as methodologically usable, subject to an explicit statement that the starting point is a declared primitive act rather than absolute nothingness.

No repair of these Bricks is currently required.

## 5. Calibration Finding

The earliest layers are not the main source of hidden arithmetic. The more serious risk begins when the construction is described in terms of exact multiplicity and comparison of finite histories.

Proceed to `CAL-EXP-002`: audit B-005 through B-008, focusing on aggregation, uniqueness, and the phrase "one new token".
