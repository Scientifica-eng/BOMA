# EXP-002 — Contextual Distinguishability

**Brick / context:** `B-001`  
**Status:** Provisional

## 1. Intention / Goal

Test whether a minimal constructional context or distinction/configuration can be specified without assuming objects, sets, or number.

## 2. Experiment

Replace the primitive binary relation `D(a,b)` with a weaker formulation: there is a constructional situation in which a difference can be specified. Treat `Context` as conditions of construction, not as a pre-existing set, collection, container, or universe.

## 3. Result

The formulation avoids explicitly assuming two relata and therefore removes the immediate circularity found in `EXP-001`. However, it does not yet provide a formal mathematical object or predicate: both `Context` and the distinction act/configuration remain informal. In particular, saying that a “difference can be specified” may already presuppose a notion of specification, sameness, or repeatability that has not been constructed.

## 4. Decision

Accept the formulation only as a methodological intermediate, not as a BOMA primitive. The next attempt must identify the minimum formal content required for “specification” or “distinguishability” and test whether that content can be stated without importing object identity, multiplicity, or number.

## 5. Epistemic Status

`Provisional`

## 6. Next Cycle

Investigate the minimum formal act of specification/distinction itself, and explicitly test whether identity or repeatability is unavoidable at that level.
