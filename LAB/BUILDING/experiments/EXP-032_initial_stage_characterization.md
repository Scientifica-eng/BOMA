# EXP-032 — Characterization of the Initial Stage

**Context:** `B-010`  
**Status:** Provisional

## 1. Intention / Goal

Determine whether the initial stage `s₀` can be characterized structurally, without simply declaring it to be the number zero.

## 2. Experiment

Inspect the construction history of `s₀` and compare it with every later constructed stage.

The candidate characterization is:

> `s₀` is the designated starting stage of the construction, with no preceding extension occurrence within the construction history.

We deliberately avoid the equation `s₀ = 0`.

## 3. Result

The characterization is coherent and distinguishes the initial stage from every later stage generated by the extension process. Every later stage has a recorded predecessor, while `s₀` has none within the chosen construction history.

This gives an intrinsic **initiality property relative to the construction**:

```text
no predecessor → s₀ → s₁ → s₂ → ...
```

However, the property does not by itself establish that `s₀` is the mathematical object called zero. Calling it zero would be an additional naming/identification choice.

The experiment therefore separates two questions:

1. Can an initial stage be characterized structurally? — Yes.
2. Is that stage necessarily the number zero? — Not established.

## 4. Decision

Accept the initiality characterization as a provisional structural result and create a Brick for it.

Do not yet assign the numeral `0` as an identity claim. The next experiment should test whether a numerical interpretation of the initial stage is useful or forced by the later construction.

## 5. Epistemic Status

`Provisional structural result`

## 6. Next Cycle

Investigate whether the initial stage can serve as a canonical base object for the entire finite-stage chain, and whether the pair (initial stage, successor-like extension) yields a structure recognizable as a candidate model of the natural numbers.
