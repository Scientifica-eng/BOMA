# EXP-011 — Methodological Correction

**Brick / context:** `B-001`  
**Status:** Accepted as methodological rule; constructional consequences remain open

## 1. Intention / Goal

Correct the experimental method so that the chosen starting point is treated as a declared hypothesis, while subsequent structure is allowed to emerge from the construction rules rather than being directed toward a predetermined route to the natural numbers.

## 2. Experiment

Review `EXP-001` through `EXP-010` and test whether the sequence has begun to impose an expected path: distinction → branching → succession → natural numbers.

## 3. Result

The review shows a methodological drift risk. Several experiments were framed in terms of what the next structure should become, especially succession and eventual connection to ℕ. This can bias the experiment toward confirming a desired architecture instead of observing what follows from the declared starting point and rules.

The chosen starting point is itself a declared methodological choice, not a theorem. Once chosen, the construction should be allowed to branch, fail, require new primitives, or terminate. A later route to ℕ is a target of the overall project, not an assumption governing each local experiment.

## 4. Decision

Adopt the following methodological rule:

> Choose the starting point explicitly; thereafter, construct according to the declared rules and record what emerges. Do not require each experiment to move toward a predetermined intermediate structure.

The earlier observations about branching and successor-like progression remain valid as provisional observations, but they must not be treated as required stages of the construction.

The next experiment will therefore not be prescribed as “test infinity” or “reach succession”. It will examine the structure currently obtained and ask what construction is legitimately enabled by it under the rules.

## 5. Epistemic Status

`Accepted` — methodological rule for this experimental cycle.

## 6. Next Cycle

Return to the current constructional state after `EXP-010` and identify the least non-presuppositional next operation permitted by the declared starting point and rules, without naming its expected destination in advance.
