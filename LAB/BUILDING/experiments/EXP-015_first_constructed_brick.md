# EXP-015 — First Constructed Brick from S-001

**Brick / context:** `B-001 / S-001`  
**Status:** Provisional

## 1. Intention / Goal

Begin the actual brick-by-brick construction from the declared starting point. The purpose is not to discover a logically necessary first object, but to make the first explicit constructional choice and observe its consequences.

## 2. Experiment

Declared starting point `S-001`: an intuitionistic, pre-numerical construction context in which no natural number, completed set, primitive object, or classical excluded-middle principle is assumed.

First constructional choice: introduce a **distinction marker** `δ` as a primitive constructional act. It is not declared to be an object, number, set, proposition, or binary relation. Its only intended role is to mark that the construction has made a distinction available.

This is an explicit choice by us, not a deduction from `S-001`.

The first Brick is therefore:

```text
B-002 := a construction context equipped with one declared distinction act δ.
```

No claim is made yet that `δ` has a unique result, that it produces two objects, or that it defines equality.

## 3. Result

The construction can be stated without introducing a pair of relata or a numeral. It gives us a minimal piece of structure on which later experiments can operate.

But the construction is deliberately weak: `δ` has not yet distinguished one entity from another because no entities have been constructed. It distinguishes only in the sense that a distinction-making act has been declared.

Thus the experiment does not yet produce an object or multiplicity.

## 4. Decision

Keep `B-002` as the first experimental Brick **only as a declared constructional choice**. Do not treat the distinction marker as discovered, canonical, or mathematically forced.

The next Brick should investigate the weakest consequence of having `δ`: whether the construction can designate a result or role associated with the act without introducing an object ontology by fiat.

## 5. Epistemic Status

`Provisional` — chosen primitive, not derived result.

## 6. Next Cycle

Attempt to construct the first referable result of `δ`. If doing so necessarily requires an additional primitive (such as identity, output/result, or a rule of formation), state that choice explicitly and test it rather than hiding it in notation.
