# EXP-025 — Choice Space for Grouping Structure

**Context:** `B-005`, `EXP-023`, `EXP-024`  
**Status:** Provisional meta-experiment

## 1. Intention / Goal

Explicitly document that the current construction leaves more than one legitimate extension open, and preserve that choice space rather than silently collapsing it into one convention.

## 2. Experiment

From `B-005`, consider the unresolved comparison:

```text
G(r₁,r₂)
G(r₂,r₁)
```

The current path permits at least two extensions:

- **O:** retain order as part of the construction;
- **U:** stipulate that reversal does not change the grouping.

We do not yet choose either extension.

## 3. Result

The choice space is real and relevant. The present Bricks do not force a unique answer.

This means that the construction path should support branching without treating the existence of a branch as an error. A later experiment may select a branch because it serves the target construction better, or both branches may be explored until their consequences diverge.

The possibility of branching is therefore recorded as part of the method itself.

## 4. Decision

Preserve both alternatives as admissible provisional continuations of `B-005`.

Do not create a Brick for either alternative yet.

Record the methodological rule:

> When the current Bricks underdetermine a choice, the choice space is itself an experimental result and must be preserved until a later construction gives a reason to select, reject, or merge alternatives.

## 5. Epistemic Status

`Accepted methodological constraint / provisional`

## 6. Next Cycle

Return to the main constructional objective and test a candidate extension that preserves the already-built result while adding a new constructional outcome. The extension must be introduced explicitly and may generate further branches.
