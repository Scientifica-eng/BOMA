# EXP-010 — Iteration from Constructional Branching

**Brick / context:** `B-001`  
**Status:** Provisional

## 1. Intention / Goal

Test whether selecting a constructional path and applying the construction again yields a genuine pre-numerical notion of succession, without presupposing natural numbers.

## 2. Experiment

Take one admissible constructional role from the branching structure and apply a declared construction operation again to its resulting role/outcome. Describe the result only as the outcome of the next application of the rule; do not label stages with numerals.

## 3. Result

A directed progression can be described:

```text
starting construction
        ↓
resulting construction state
        ↓
next application
        ↓
next construction state
```

This gives a weak notion of **successor-like progression**: a construction state can be followed by a state obtained through a specified transformation.

However, the experiment does not yet establish a successor function on a domain. We have no constructed collection of states, no primitive zero, no induction principle, and no proof that repeated application is always defined or produces distinguishable states.

A further issue is that “next” is currently supplied by the declared construction operation. Thus succession has not emerged from nothing; it is inherited from an explicitly chosen operation.

## 4. Decision

Accept **successor-like progression generated by a declared construction operation** as a provisional pre-numerical structure. Do not identify it with the natural-number successor function.

This is an important boundary: the construction has now reached an operation that can generate ordered stages, but we must still determine the minimum conditions under which the generated stages form a stable sequence rather than merely a finite or partial process.

## 5. Epistemic Status

`Provisional`

## 6. Next Cycle

Test whether the construction operation can be iterated indefinitely in principle, and whether its stages remain distinguishable and non-circular without introducing natural-number induction as an assumption.
