# EXP-001 — B-001 Minimal Distinction

**Brick / context:** `B-001`  
**Status:** Provisional

## 1. Intention / Goal

Find the weakest intuitionistic starting structure that permits distinction without presupposing natural numbers.

## 2. Experiment

Tried to take `D(a,b)` — distinguishability of two relata — as the primitive form of distinction.

## 3. Result

The formulation already presupposes `a` and `b`, whose status has not yet been established. It therefore imports unexplained relata into the pre-numerical foundation.

## 4. Decision

Do not accept `D(a,b)` as the first primitive. Move one level down and investigate whether a constructional context/act of distinguishability can be specified before identifying its relata.

## 5. Epistemic Status

`Provisional`

## 6. Next Cycle

Test whether a minimal constructional context or distinction/configuration can be specified without assuming objects, sets, or number.
