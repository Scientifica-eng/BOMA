# B-001 — Minimal Distinction

**Cycle:** `PDCA-001`  
**Stage:** Pre-numerical foundation  
**Status:** Construction attempt / provisional  
**Starting-point decision:** `S-001`

## Construction question

What is the least intuitionistically acceptable structure that permits a distinction without presupposing natural numbers?

The target is deliberately **not** a first number, a set, or a completed mathematical universe.

The immediate target is a minimal distinction from which later constructions may become possible.

## Starting constraints

We begin under the following declared constraints:

1. The logical baseline is intuitionistic.
2. Natural numbers are not primitive.
3. Peano axioms are not primitive.
4. Classical excluded middle is not assumed.
5. A completed set-theoretic universe is not assumed.
6. `Context` is not yet identified with a set, collection, container, or universe of objects.
7. A first `Object` is not assumed as primitive merely because we want one.

## Initial hypothesis

A distinction is more primitive for the present experiment than an isolated object.

Tentative notation:

```text
D(a,b)
```

read only as:

> `a` and `b` are distinguishable in the relevant construction context.

At this stage this notation is **informal experimental notation**, not a defined BOMA predicate.

## First difficulty discovered

The notation `D(a,b)` already appears to presuppose two terms, `a` and `b`.

Therefore the phrase “minimal distinction” cannot yet be treated as a completely primitive binary relation unless we also explain where its relata come from.

This exposes a foundational fork:

```text
Option A:
  objects/terms are available first
  → distinction is a relation between them

Option B:
  a primitive act/configuration of distinction is available
  → relata are identified within that act/configuration
```

Option B is closer to the intended experiment because Option A risks importing the very object ontology we are trying to minimize.

## Revised working formulation

For this construction attempt, we therefore do **not** accept `D(a,b)` as the first primitive.

Instead we investigate a weaker notion:

> There is a constructional situation in which a difference can be specified.

Call this provisionally:

```text
Distinguishability(C, δ)
```

where:

- `C` is the not-yet-formalized construction context;
- `δ` is the not-yet-formalized distinction act/configuration.

Neither symbol is currently admitted into the final BOMA ontology.

## What has actually been established

Only the following methodological result has been obtained so far:

```text
The attempt to make a binary distinction relation primitive
immediately raises the question of where its relata come from.
```

This is a genuine constructional constraint, not yet a mathematical theorem.

## Negative result

We reject, for the present experiment, the premature definition:

```text
D(a,b) := primitive
```

because it leaves `a` and `b` unexplained.

This rejection is provisional and may be reversed if a later construction demonstrates that terms/objects can be admitted without compromising the pre-numerical objective.

## Candidate next construction

The next attempt should investigate whether the following can be made precise without importing number:

```text
construction context
        ↓
possible distinction/configuration
        ↓
identified relata
```

Only after that should we ask whether the relata deserve the status of `object`, `entity`, `term`, or another primitive.

## Relation to identity

It is tempting to move immediately from distinction to identity:

```text
a = a
```

This is intentionally postponed.

If identity is introduced as primitive, we must determine whether this is an unavoidable logical background notion or an additional mathematical commitment. In intuitionistic logic, identity/equality has its own proof-theoretic role and should not be conflated with classical bivalence.

## Relation to multiplicity

The distinction experiment is potentially the first place where something resembling multiplicity can arise, because distinguishability appears to require a difference.

But we do **not** yet infer:

```text
difference → two
```

because the concept of “two” is precisely part of the target pre-numerical construction.

## Evidence

No formal proof artifact has yet been produced.

The current evidence is a **constructional analysis** showing that a naive primitive formulation creates an immediate dependency on unexplained relata.

Therefore the status is:

```text
Evidence: conceptual
Verification: not formalized
Guarantee: none
```

## Lessons for the next attempt

1. Do not introduce `D(a,b)` until the status of `a` and `b` is accounted for.
2. Do not define `Context` as a set merely to make the notation convenient.
3. Do not introduce “two” implicitly when describing a distinction.
4. Keep object, term, identity, and distinction conceptually separate until the construction forces a relation between them.
5. Treat every new primitive as a declared choice requiring justification.

## PDCA classification

**Plan:** construct the weakest plausible pre-numerical distinction.

**Do:** attempt to formulate distinction directly as a primitive binary relation.

**Check:** the formulation presupposes two relata whose status has not been established.

**Act:** reject the binary relation as the first primitive and move one level down to the constructional conditions/act of distinguishability.
