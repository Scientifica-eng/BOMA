# REBUILD-EXP-001 — Rebuilding B-001

**Cycle:** `REBUILD-001`  
**Target:** `B-001`  
**Status:** Provisional decision

## 1. Intention / Goal

Reconstruct the first Brick from the calibrated context, without importing numerical concepts, sets, equality, identity, order, or a ready-made logical structure.

## 2. Starting Context

The project does not claim absolute nothingness. It begins with a condition of possibility:

> A difference can be registered within the context in which construction takes place.

This statement is treated as contextual, not as a constructed object and not as a Brick.

## 3. Experiment

### Step A — Registerable difference

There is a contrast sufficient for the constructional mechanism to respond differently to two sides/aspects of the context.

We deliberately avoid saying that there are already "two objects". The contrast may be figure/background, signal/non-signal, or any analogous distinction-producing situation.

### Step B — Possibility of distinction

Because the contrast can be registered, the constructional mechanism can select one side/aspect rather than treating the entire context as undifferentiated.

This is an operational capability, not a numerical comparison.

### Step C — Distinction `δ`

`δ` is stipulated as the first explicit constructive act:

> **perform a distinction on a registerable difference.**

The result is not yet called an element, set, number, or object in a formal mathematical sense.

### Step D — Immediate result

The act leaves a constructional trace: something has been selected/marked in a way that can potentially be referred to by subsequent construction.

For the moment we denote this trace by `r`, purely as a temporary symbol.

## 4. Result

The first Brick can be stated without numerical terminology as:

```text
registerable difference
        ↓
possibility of distinction
        ↓
δ: distinction
        ↓
constructional trace r
```

The crucial result is not the creation of a mathematically individuated object. It is the creation of a **persistent constructional trace produced by an explicit act**.

## 5. What Has Not Been Assumed

The experiment does not require:

- natural numbers;
- counting;
- cardinality;
- sets or set membership;
- an equality relation;
- an ordering relation;
- successor;
- induction;
- two pre-existing mathematical objects.

The symbols `δ` and `r` are notation for the constructional act and its trace; they are not yet mathematical objects with the usual formal semantics.

## 6. Remaining Vulnerability

The phrase "persistent constructional trace" contains an unresolved commitment: what makes the trace the same trace when it is referred to again?

We therefore must **not** smuggle identity into B-001. The problem of referability belongs to the next experimental step.

Similarly, saying that the act "selects one side" is operational language. It must not be converted into a cardinality statement.

## 7. Decision

**B-001 is provisionally accepted for the repaired path.**

Its role is:

> the first explicit constructive act transforming a registerable difference into a constructional trace.

No claim is made that this is an absolute metaphysical beginning.

## 8. Next

Do not yet declare `B-002` complete. The next experiment must determine what is minimally required for the constructional trace `r` to be referable again without importing identity/equality.
