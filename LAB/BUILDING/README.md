# BOMA Building Kit

**Status:** Experimental working infrastructure  
**Cycle:** `PDCA-001`  
**Purpose:** Keep the mathematical construction navigable, auditable, and recoverable while the project moves from the pre-numerical foundation toward the natural numbers.

## Operating rule

The building is the experiment. These files are instruments for recording and navigating it; they are not normative BOMA law.

## Files

- `ROADMAP.md` — current objectives, gates, and next-step discipline.
- `BRICK_TEMPLATE.md` — template for a standalone mathematical Brick record.
- `CONSTRUCTION_LOG_TEMPLATE.md` — record of actual construction attempts, including failures and corrections.
- `DECISION_RECORD_TEMPLATE.md` — explicit record for foundational choices and rejected alternatives.
- `CALIBRATION_CHECKLIST.md` — periodic self-audit against the project's goal and epistemic discipline.
- `STATUS.md` — compact current-state dashboard and recovery point.

## Working principles

1. The trunk uses intuitionistic logic by default.
2. Any departure from the trunk logic requires explicit necessity, alternatives, minimality, transparency, and traceability.
3. Dependency is not automatically required for every Brick; independence is allowed when compatible and useful.
4. Non-conflict is necessary for admissibility, but conflict must be evaluated relative to the declared logical context.
5. Structural dependency, logical requirement, evidence, guarantee, context, and policy remain distinct until construction demonstrates that they should be combined.
6. A failed construction attempt is retained as evidence; it is not silently erased.
7. A provisional observation is not promoted to a normative rule without further evidence.
8. We do not pre-commit to a binary `intuitionistic/classical` universe of logics.
9. The mathematical content remains primary; the BOMA architecture serves the construction rather than becoming its substitute.

## Recovery rule

If the project becomes unclear, stop adding Bricks and consult, in order:

```text
STATUS.md
→ ROADMAP.md
→ latest Brick record
→ latest Construction Log
→ latest Decision Record
→ latest Calibration
```

The objective is to make it possible to resume the experiment without relying on conversational memory alone.
