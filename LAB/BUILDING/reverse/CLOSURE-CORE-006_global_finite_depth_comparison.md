# CLOSURE-CORE-006 — Global Comparison of Finite Construction Depths

## Status
Exploratory, provisional.

## Purpose
Test whether all finite generated histories admit a stable global comparison of construction depth without first postulating a completed mathematical totality.

## Construction
Each finite history is reduced to the initial carrier by repeated removal of elementary extensions. Depth comparison is therefore induced operationally by the number of reversible extension/removal steps in the construction record.

## Findings
1. Any two already-present finite histories can be compared through their finite construction records.
2. Comparison can be performed by structural decomposition rather than by assigning prior numerical labels.
3. The resulting ordering behavior is coherent across the finite horizon, provided the same elementary extension protocol is used.
4. This remains an operational comparison of finite histories; it is not yet a global order relation on a completed mathematical domain.

## Boundary
The finite horizon can therefore support stable depth comparison without requiring a completed totality. What remains unresolved is whether the collection of all such finite depths can be promoted to one completed domain without an additional foundational commitment.
