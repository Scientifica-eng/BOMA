# REVERSE-019 — Unlabeled Pattern Chain and Its Boundary

**Cycle:** `REVERSE-001`  
**Status:** Provisional result

## Intention / Goal

Determine whether the structural patterns obtained after provenance removal can form a coherent chain from an initial pattern by repeated transition, without numerical labels or imported mathematical theories.

## Experiment

Start from a distinguished initial pattern `P₀` and apply the transition inherited from the construction protocol:

```text
P₀ → P₁ → P₂ → P₃ → ...
```

Do not assign numbers to the patterns. Do not define them as members of a set. Do not assume a pre-existing order relation.

Test only the following:

1. whether an initial pattern can be singled out by absence of a prior transition of the same kind;
2. whether the transition produces a recognizable successor-pattern role;
3. whether repeated transition preserves the same type of structural organization;
4. whether distinct histories can converge to the same structural pattern without numerical comparison.

## Result

The experiment supports a coherent unlabeled pattern chain generated by the transition itself. The chain can be described operationally and structurally without numerical indexing.

The initial pattern is identifiable through its constructional role, and each later pattern is identifiable through its transition role relative to the preceding pattern.

However, the experiment does not establish that every possible carrier belongs to this chain, nor that the chain is complete, nor that the chain is the final mathematical domain.

## Key Finding

We can distinguish:

```text
unlabeled generated pattern chain
        !=
formal natural-number domain
```

The first can be obtained without numbers. The second still requires a rule specifying what counts as the complete domain and how structurally indistinguishable patterns are treated.

## Decision

Accept the unlabeled pattern chain as a legitimate pre-numerical structure for further experiments.

Do not rename its patterns `0, 1, 2, ...`.

Do not yet introduce a formal equality relation, set membership, or induction principle.

## Next Question

Can the initial pattern be characterized solely by the constructional protocol, and can the chain's domain be fixed by the protocol without adding an external completeness principle?
