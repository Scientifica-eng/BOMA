# EXTERNAL-INTERPRETATION-001 — Candidate Mathematical Frameworks

## Status

Phase 8 — External mathematical interpretation.

This document deliberately does **not** declare that the pre-formal construction is already ℕ. It records external mathematical structures that can serve as interpretations of the structure obtained through the reverse-engineering path.

## Pre-formal structure being interpreted

The construction developed so far supplies, at minimum:

- a distinguished initial structural role;
- an elementary extension/transition role;
- finite histories generated by repeated elementary extension;
- composition of histories;
- operational associativity of sequential composition;
- a candidate empty history as identity, when explicitly admitted;
- construction depth as a structural invariant;
- finite decomposition into elementary extensions;
- local referability without a prior global mathematical domain.

These are observations from the construction. They are **not** yet statements that a set, equality relation, natural-number object, or induction principle exists.

## Candidate A — Additive monoid / free monoid on one generator

A standard external interpretation is the additive monoid of non-negative integers, with addition and zero. The natural numbers including zero form a free monoid on one generator in the usual algebraic sense: the generator can correspond to one elementary step, finite composition corresponds to addition, and the empty word corresponds to zero.

The fit is strongest for history composition, associativity, a candidate identity, one elementary generator, and construction depth.

What this interpretation adds is formal objects, an operation, an identity, and the universal/free characterization. Those are not supplied merely by observing finite construction histories.

The phrase “free monoid on one generator” is therefore an **external identification**, not a premise of the construction.

## Candidate B — Peano-style successor structure

A second external interpretation is a natural-number structure with an initial element and successor operation. Standard Peano-style presentations include an initial natural number, closure under successor, successor non-origin, injectivity of successor, and an induction principle; exact presentations vary with the logical framework.

The fit is strongest for the initial role, elementary extension/successor, predecessor/decomposition of finite stages, and the distinction between finite generation and complete totality.

The decisive added commitment is a global principle such as induction or an equivalent completeness condition. This corresponds directly to the closure problem isolated in the CLOSURE-CORE series.

## Candidate C — Initial algebra / inductive datatype interpretation

A third interpretation treats the structure as an initial algebra generated by one base constructor and one unary successor-like constructor. This is conceptually close to the project's construction because it separates local constructors from the global object they generate.

Its fit is strongest for base-plus-extension, finite generation, recursive decomposition, structural recursion, and canonical finite construction shape.

The notion of initial algebra is itself mathematical infrastructure, so it remains an external interpretation rather than a hidden premise.

## Candidate D — Category-theoretic natural-number object

A category-theoretic interpretation can represent the natural-number structure as an object equipped with zero-like and successor-like maps satisfying a suitable universal property.

This is useful for separating the structure from the maps acting on it and from the universal property that identifies the intended object.

Its cost is the introduction of categorical infrastructure and a universal-property criterion.

## Comparative result

The current evidence does **not** select one framework uniquely.

| Candidate | Fit to observed construction | Main extra commitment |
|---|---|---|
| Additive/free monoid | Very strong | formal monoid + identity + freeness |
| Peano successor structure | Very strong | formal domain + induction/completeness |
| Initial algebra | Very strong | algebraic/category-theoretic initiality |
| Natural-number object | Strong | categorical infrastructure + universal property |

The correct Phase-8 conclusion is therefore **underdetermination**:

> The pre-formal construction has enough structure to admit multiple mathematically respectable interpretations. It should not be declared to “be” ℕ merely because one interpretation is available.

## Required next comparison

The next question is not “which theory is true?” It is:

> **Which formal commitments are necessary and sufficient to map the observed pre-formal structure into each candidate interpretation?**

The comparison should focus on identity of stages, global closure/completeness, composition/addition, and uniqueness/canonicity.

## Prohibited shortcut

> “The construction looks like the free monoid on one generator, therefore it is ℕ.”

This inference is rejected. The permitted procedure is:

> construct → isolate structural properties → compare against external theories → record added commitments → identify the resulting formal structure.
