# PHASE-9-002 — Free-Monoid Formal Matching

## Status

Phase 9 — Formal matching against the natural-number target.

## Purpose

Test the construction under an explicitly declared free-monoid formalization. This is a conditional interpretation, not a claim that the free-monoid framework is the project's native or uniquely justified foundation.

## Formalization choice for this test

For this test only, introduce a formal monoid of finite construction histories, with:

- an identity represented by the empty history;
- associative composition of histories;
- one distinguished elementary-extension generator;
- finite generation by composition from that generator.

The freeness condition is treated as an additional framework-specific requirement, not as an observation inherited automatically from the pre-formal construction.

## Matching map

```text
empty construction history       <-> monoid identity
one elementary extension         <-> distinguished generator
history composition              <-> monoid operation
finite construction history      <-> finite word in the generator
construction depth               <-> generator-occurrence count
```

## Conditional result

Under this formalization, finite construction histories map coherently to the finite words generated by one elementary symbol. Composition of histories corresponds to monoid multiplication, and the constructional depth agrees with the number of elementary-extension occurrences in the corresponding finite word.

If the formal monoid is additionally declared free on that generator, its elements are canonically represented by finite generator words. Under the standard external interpretation, the additive monoid of non-negative integers is isomorphic to this free monoid, with generator-occurrence count corresponding to addition.

## What is not proved

This test does not prove that:

1. the free-monoid framework is forced by the pre-formal construction;
2. the existence of a formal monoid is derived from the neutral layer;
3. the universal/free property is pre-formally established;
4. the project has a framework-independent derivation of the natural numbers;
5. the finite-history structure by itself supplies a completed global domain.

## Boundary finding

```text
neutral construction
        |
        v
finite histories + composition + candidate identity
        |
        v
explicit free-monoid formalization
        |
        v
formal monoid + identity + generator + freeness
        |
        v
external isomorphism with the additive natural-number monoid
```

The decisive imported ingredients are the formal monoid structure and, more strongly, the freeness/universal-property claim. The neutral construction supplies strong behavioral evidence for the ingredients but does not by itself establish their formal mathematical status.

## Comparison point with PHASE-9-001

The Peano-style matching and free-monoid matching share the same constructional correspondences for initial/elementary-extension structure and finite generation. They differ in where the framework places the main global commitment:

- Peano: formal domain plus closure/induction.
- Free monoid: formal monoid plus identity/composition plus freeness.

This difference is framework-specific and must not be retroactively attributed to the pre-formal layer.

## Rule for Phase 9

Every theorem obtained through a framework-specific axiom or structural principle remains explicitly conditional on that framework. No framework-specific result is promoted to a pre-formal fact.
