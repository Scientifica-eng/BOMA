## What the audit confirms

### 1. No hidden arithmetic primitive was discovered

No new primitive constructional ingredient was needed for addition, multiplication, or order beyond the previously established extension, finite history, composition, and reachability structures.

### 2. The formalization boundary is stable

The following remain on the formal side of the boundary:

```text

```

These have not been retroactively attributed to the pre-formal layer.

### 3. The reverse-engineered target and forward construction agree

The two routes converge on the same finite generative structure, providing independent support for internal structural coherence.

## What the project has NOT established

The project has not established any of the following framework-free claims:

- that the chosen pre-formal starting perspective is mathematically necessary;
- that the common finite generative core uniquely determines the completed natural-number object;
- that Peano realization is uniquely forced;
- that the global completion principle is derivable from the finite construction alone;
- that a universal characterization can be stated without a semantic framework;
- that the project has derived the existence of a globally completed N without a constitutive formalization choice.
