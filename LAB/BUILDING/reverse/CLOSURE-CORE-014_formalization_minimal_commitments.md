# CLOSURE-CORE-014 — Minimal Formalization Commitments

## Status

Provisional experimental result.

## Question

What is the weakest explicit commitment required when moving from the locally referable finite construction horizon to a formal mathematical domain?

## Result

The transition is better decomposed into distinct commitments rather than treated as one leap:

1. **Objectification** — construction patterns may be treated as stable references.
2. **Cross-reference** — references originating from different finite constructions may be coordinated.
3. **Global domain recognition** — those references are taken as belonging to one formal domain.
4. **Formal identity** — a mathematical identity criterion is selected for the domain.
5. **Formal operations** — operations such as extension and composition receive formal status.
6. **Framework choice** — a mathematical foundation is chosen (set-theoretic, type-theoretic, categorical, or another suitable framework).

The experiments to this point show that (1) can be separated from (3), and that the finite operational layer can be maintained without (3)–(6).

## Boundary finding

The project can therefore postpone the strongest commitments until a deliberate formalization boundary. The currently identified boundary is not yet “choose set theory” or “declare N.” It is the explicit decision to promote the constructionally characterized reference structure into a formally governed mathematical domain.

## Important limitation

This experiment does **not** prove that the listed commitments are logically independent in every possible foundation. It only establishes a useful methodological decomposition for the present neutral construction path.
