# CLOSURE-CORE-009 — Object Reference Assembly Without Declared Totality

Status: experimental

## Question

Can minimally objectified finite construction references be assembled into one reusable structural arrangement without yet declaring a mathematical set, class, type, or totality?

## Construction

Starting from the finite horizon established in CORE-007 and the minimal objectification in CORE-008, each accepted finite pattern is treated as a stable reference. We then require only that references can be related by the already established structural relations:

- initial role;
- elementary extension role;
- composition of finite histories;
- recoverable construction depth.

The experiment explicitly avoids introducing set membership, cardinality, Peano axioms, or a general equality relation.

## Result

A reusable **reference arrangement** can be specified operationally: references are available as targets for the same structural operations, and finite transitions remain recoverable. This gives a coherent working structure stronger than isolated objects but weaker than a declared mathematical totality.

The distinction is:

```text
individual finite reference
        ↓
reusable reference arrangement
        ≠
mathematical totality containing all references
```

The experiment therefore lowers the amount of commitment needed for practical reuse, but it does not remove the final completion question.

## Boundary

The moment we say that the reference arrangement is *the* complete domain of all relevant objects, a new global commitment is introduced. That statement is not supplied by the local construction rules themselves.

## Consequence

The current evidence supports a three-step separation:

1. finite structural production;
2. minimal objectification and reusable arrangement;
3. optional global completion into a formal mathematical domain.

The third step must remain explicit rather than being smuggled in through terminology.
