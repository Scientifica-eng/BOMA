# FINAL-SYNTHESIS-001 — Stages 1–10 Synthesis and Decision Record

## Status

Final synthesis after completion of Stage 10 integration.

## Scope

This document consolidates the project results from the pre-formal starting point through the reverse-engineering path, external interpretation, formal matching, and forward/reverse integration.

## Strongest defensible project claim

The project has identified a coherent pre-formal constructional core that can be described without taking the natural numbers themselves as primitive:

```text
minimal distinguishability
        ↓
distinguished initial role
        ↓
repeatable elementary extension
        ↓
finite construction history
        ↓
construction depth
        ↓
composition / accumulation
        ↓
recoverability / decomposition
        ↓
successor-chain behavior
```

Independent forward and reverse investigations converge on this same finite generative pattern.

Under an explicitly selected Peano-style formal realization, this core embeds coherently into a complete natural-number structure:

```text
initial role       ↔ 0
extension          ↔ successor
history depth      ↔ natural-number stage
history composition↔ addition
iterated composition ↔ multiplication
reachability       ↔ order
```

Thus the project has demonstrated a coherent constructional provenance for the standard natural-number operations within the selected formal realization.

## Claims the project does NOT establish

The project does not establish that:

1. the chosen pre-formal starting perspective is mathematically necessary;
2. the finite constructional core by itself forces a unique completed mathematical object;
3. Peano is the uniquely correct or uniquely forced formal framework;
4. global completion can be derived from the finite construction without an additional constitutive commitment;
5. a framework-neutral universal property supplying existence, completeness, and canonicity has been discovered;
6. the project has derived the existence of a globally completed natural-number universe independently of a formalization choice.

## Constitutive boundary

The currently irreducible constitutive boundary is:

```text
PRE-FORMAL CONSTRUCTION
    ↓
choose a formal realization regime
    ↓
formal domain + identity/equivalence + completion rule
    ↓
formal natural-number structure
```

For the integration experiments, the selected regime was Peano-style. This was a project choice for formal realization, not a theorem derived from the pre-formal layer.

## Stage-10 outcome

Stage 10 is complete **conditionally under the declared Peano-style realization**.

The forward and reverse paths are integrated at the finite structural level, the arithmetic operations have traceable constructional provenance, and no new primitive constructional axiom was required to reach addition, multiplication, or order.

## Decision

The project should not continue indefinitely searching for a framework-free derivation of full N without first accepting an explicit constitutive boundary. The next substantive work should therefore be one of the following, chosen explicitly:

```text
A. deepen the minimal constitutive choice;
B. compare the completed Peano realization against the other tested frameworks;
C. begin the next number-system construction while preserving the same boundary discipline.
```

No later result may retroactively convert a constitutive choice into a pre-formal theorem.
