# REVERSE-035 — Construction Depth Under Composition

**Status:** Experimental result — provisional  
**Plan stage:** Reverse engineering → algebraic candidate validation → depth invariant test

## Question

Does the construction depth discovered in REVERSE-032 become a stable structural quantity once the compositional behavior tested in REVERSE-034 is taken into account?

We deliberately avoid calling the depth a number and avoid importing arithmetic operations.

## Setup

A construction history has:

- an initial role;
- a sequence of elementary extensions;
- a compositional operation on histories;
- an observed depth relative to the initial role.

Schematically:

```text
empty-like history      → depth at the initial role
one extension            → next depth state
repeated extension       → deeper state
A followed by B          → composed history
```

## Test 1 — Extension changes depth in one stable step

For a history H and one admissible elementary extension E:

```text
H → H·E
```

The observed depth changes by one successor-like transition.

This is not yet arithmetic addition of 1. It is only a repeatable local change in the depth invariant.

## Test 2 — Composition of histories

For histories A and B, composition gives:

```text
A·B
```

The depth of the composite behaves as the result of continuing the depth represented by A through the extension sequence represented by B.

This establishes a qualitative additive-composition pattern:

```text
Depth(A·B) = continuation_of(Depth(A), B)
```

We do **not** yet rewrite this as a numerical equation such as depth(A)+depth(B).

## Test 3 — Independence from provenance

When construction provenance is removed while the structural extension pattern is preserved, the observed depth pattern remains available.

Therefore the depth is not tied to the historical identity of the carrier. It is attached to the preserved structural role of the history.

## Test 4 — Stability under representation-preserving changes

Changing the presentation of the same preserved extension pattern does not change the observed depth transition pattern.

This is consistent with the earlier representation-neutrality results, but it does not eliminate the underlying comparison operation. The comparison remains operational: the relevant extension behavior must remain recoverable.

## Provisional result

The experiments support the following claims:

1. Construction depth is a stable invariant of the preserved extension pattern.
2. A single elementary extension produces a repeatable successor-like change in depth.
3. History composition induces a composition law on depth behavior.
4. This law is not yet justified as numerical addition.
5. The depth invariant can survive removal of provenance when the structural transition pattern is preserved.
6. No numerical labels are required for these claims.

## What has NOT been established

We have **not** established that:

- the depths form the natural numbers;
- the depth values constitute a complete domain;
- the empty-like history must be admitted as a neutral element without an explicit modeling choice;
- the depth-composition law is an arithmetic operation;
- the resulting structure is formally a free monoid;
- an infinite totality of all depth states exists as a completed mathematical object.

## Interpretation

The present best description is:

> Construction depth behaves as a compositional structural invariant generated by repeatable extension.

This is stronger than merely saying that a chain looks successor-like, but weaker than saying that we have already constructed ℕ.

The next question is whether the depth invariant plus the compositional behavior uniquely determines a familiar abstract structure, or whether an additional foundational choice is still required.
