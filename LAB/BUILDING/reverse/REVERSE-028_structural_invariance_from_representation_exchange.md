# REVERSE-028 — Representation Exchange as a Precursor to Order Neutrality

**Status:** Experiment completed — provisional result  
**Scope:** REVERSE-027 and pre-count invariance

## 1. Intention / Goal

Test whether order-neutrality can arise from a more primitive principle than arithmetic commutativity: namely, that different presentations of the same structural configuration may be exchanged without changing the structural result.

## 2. Experiment

Consider two presentations of one externally distinguished plurality:

```text
Presentation A: a, b, c
Presentation B: c, a, b
```

The question is not whether A and B are "equal" as mathematical objects. That concept is intentionally unavailable.

Instead ask whether the construction protocol can treat them as **interchangeable presentations with respect to the structural feature being measured**.

The test was applied to the pre-count correspondence process:

```text
external distinguishable plurality
        ↓
correspondence with pattern-chain positions
        ↓
structural result
```

## 3. Result

A representation-exchange principle can preserve the structural result without using the arithmetic notion of commutativity.

The principle is weaker and more general:

> **Changing only the presentation path, while preserving the structural configuration relevant to the correspondence, does not change the structural invariant being extracted.**

This is not yet a formal theorem of invariance. It is a protocol criterion.

## 4. Boundary

Representation exchange does not by itself prove that every reordering is structurally irrelevant. The protocol must specify what information is preserved under exchange.

Therefore:

```text
arbitrary reorder
    ≠
structure-preserving exchange
```

The latter is the defensible notion at this stage.

## 5. Consequence for Pre-Counting

If two presentation paths preserve the same structural configuration, then the resulting pattern-chain endpoint can be treated as a presentation-independent feature of the correspondence.

Thus the path becomes:

```text
presentation
   ↓
structure-preserving correspondence
   ↓
exchange of presentation
   ↓
preserved invariant
```

This strengthens the possibility of defining a pre-count invariant without introducing natural numbers.

## 6. What Has Not Been Established

The experiment does not establish:

- arithmetic commutativity;
- cardinality;
- equality of arbitrary presentations;
- a permutation group;
- a quotient construction;
- the existence of natural numbers;
- uniqueness of the invariant under all possible transformations.

## 7. Decision

1. Accept **structure-preserving representation exchange** as a provisional protocol principle.
2. Do not import the term "commutativity" into the pre-mathematical layer.
3. Continue to treat the invariant as a candidate structural quantity, not a number.
4. Require any future claim of order-neutrality to identify which structural information is preserved by the exchange.

## 8. Next Experiment

Test whether repeated structure-preserving exchanges generate a stable class of presentations, and whether that class can be described without importing formal equivalence, permutation, or cardinality theory.

The target question is:

> **Can presentation-independence itself be constructed as a stable behavioral property before formal mathematical equality is chosen?**
