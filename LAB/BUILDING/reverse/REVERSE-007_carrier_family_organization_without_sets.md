# REVERSE-007 — Organizing Referable Carriers Without Predefined Sets

**Status:** Experiment completed — provisional result  
**Previous:** `REVERSE-006`

## 1. Intention / Goal

Test whether referable constructional carriers can be organized into a coherent structure without first declaring a set, membership relation, or numerical cardinality.

## 2. Starting Point

From `REVERSE-006` we have candidate carriers produced by constructional histories. Each carrier is:

- generated by the construction protocol;
- referable within the protocol;
- connected to its generating history;
- not yet declared to be a mathematical set element.

## 3. Experiment

We test whether the carriers can be handled through three constructional roles only:

1. **availability:** a carrier has been produced and is available for later construction;
2. **transition:** a carrier may be transformed/extended according to the protocol;
3. **provenance:** the construction records from which carrier/stage another was obtained.

No statement of the form

```text
x ∈ X
```

is introduced.

No cardinality is assigned to the collection of carriers.

No claim is made that all possible carriers already exist as a completed totality.

## 4. Result

A coherent organization is possible at the protocol level. We can speak of a family of currently available carriers by referring to the constructional history that produces and preserves them, without first treating that family as a completed set.

The organization can therefore be represented schematically as:

```text
initial carrier
      │
      ├── transition → next carrier
      │
      ├── transition → next carrier
      │
      └── provenance links record the construction path
```

This is weaker than a mathematical collection with primitive membership.

The experiment shows that **organization precedes set-membership** in the current route: we can have a structured domain of available constructional carriers before declaring the domain to be a set in the formal mathematical sense.

## 5. Limitation

The expression "family of carriers" remains descriptive. To turn it into a formal mathematical domain, we will eventually need an explicit criterion for what counts as an admissible carrier and what totality, if any, the domain possesses.

Thus we have not eliminated the foundational choice; we have localized it.

The likely boundary is now:

```text
constructional organization
        ↓
[formalization choice]
        ↓
mathematical domain
```

## 6. Decision

Accept the protocol-level organization of referable carriers as a valid pre-mathematical structure.

Do not yet introduce set membership.

Record that **organization/availability/provenance can precede the formal notion of a set** in this construction.

The next experiment should test whether the transition relation on carriers is sufficient to recover a distinguished initial carrier and a successor-like operation without a separately imposed ordering relation.

## 7. Epistemic Status

**Provisional structural result.**
