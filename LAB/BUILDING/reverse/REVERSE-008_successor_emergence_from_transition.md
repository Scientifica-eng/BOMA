# REVERSE-008 — Successor-Like Relation Emerging from Transition

**Status:** Provisional result  
**Context:** `REVERSE-007`

## 1. Intention / Goal

Test whether a successor-like relation between referable carriers can arise from the transition operation itself, without importing an independent order relation.

## 2. Experiment

Start with a referable carrier `c` obtained from the generated construction and apply the admissible transition protocol `E`:

```text
c ──E──► c⁺
```

Ask whether the construction itself provides enough information to distinguish:

- the carrier before the transition;
- the carrier produced by the transition;
- and the direct transition relation between them.

No independent ordering relation `<` is introduced.

## 3. Result

The transition occurrence itself supplies a directed local relation from the prior carrier to the newly produced carrier.

The resulting structure can therefore be represented schematically as:

```text
c₀ ──E──► c₁ ──E──► c₂ ──E──► c₃
```

The direction is inherited from the constructional operation, not imposed by a separate ordering primitive.

However, the experiment does **not** show that this relation is already a total order, a well-order, or the formal successor function of arithmetic. It is only a **successor-like transition relation** generated by the construction.

## 4. Important Limitation

The distinction between `c` and `c⁺` relies on the transition protocol producing a new constructional result. It does not yet supply a general theory of equality, inequality, or numerical successor.

Moreover, the experiment does not establish that every carrier belongs to one unique chain, nor that every admissible carrier has a predecessor/successor in a global mathematical domain.

## 5. Decision

Accept the successor-like transition as a **derived structural relation** on generated carriers.

Do not create a new foundational primitive for order.

Do not identify the relation with the arithmetic successor function yet.

## 6. Significance for the Two Paths

This result strengthens the possible convergence of the forward and reverse paths:

```text
FORWARD                         REVERSE
extension E                     target successor structure
    ↓                               ↓
new carrier                      successor-like relation
    ↓                               ↓
local predecessor/successor  ← convergence point →
```

The relation appears to be generated by the same constructional transition that the forward path had introduced explicitly.

## 7. Next Question

Test whether the generated carrier structure has a unique initial carrier under the same constructional protocol, and whether all generated carriers are connected to that initial carrier by their construction history without using numerical labels.
