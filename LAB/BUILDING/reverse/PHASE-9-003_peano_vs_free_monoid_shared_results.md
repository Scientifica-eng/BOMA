# PHASE-9-003 — Shared Results of Peano and Free-Monoid Matching

## Status

Phase 9 — formal matching against the natural-number target.

## Purpose

Compare the two explicit formal matches already performed in PHASE-9-001 and PHASE-9-002. The goal is to distinguish what survives both formalizations from what is introduced by the chosen framework.

## Common constructional results

Both formal matches preserve the following correspondences from the neutral construction:

- a distinguished initial structural role;
- one elementary extension/transition role;
- finite construction histories generated by repeated elementary extension;
- decomposition of finite histories by reversing elementary extensions;
- construction depth as an invariant of the finite history;
- sequential composition of histories;
- a coherent correspondence between composition of histories and accumulation of finite stages.

These results occur in both formalizations and therefore have stronger status within the project than framework-specific consequences.

## Peano-specific additions

The Peano-style match explicitly introduces:

- a formal carrier N;
- a distinguished element 0;
- a successor operation S;
- a closure/induction principle sufficient to identify the intended total domain.

The natural-number interpretation is conditional on these additions.

## Free-monoid-specific additions

The free-monoid match explicitly introduces:

- a formal monoid carrier of histories;
- an identity represented by the empty history;
- a formal associative binary operation for history composition;
- a distinguished generator for the elementary extension;
- the freeness/universal-property condition.

The identification with the additive monoid of non-negative integers is an external mathematical identification of the resulting free monoid.

## Comparison

| Feature | Common to both matches | Peano-specific | Free-monoid-specific |
|---|---|---|---|
| Initial role | Yes | Formalized as 0 | Indirectly related to identity/empty history |
| Elementary extension | Yes | Formalized as S | Formalized as generator |
| Finite generation | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Finite decomposition | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Construction depth | Yes | Mapped to stage index | Mapped to generator-occurrence count |
| History composition | Yes | Interpreted via stage composition | Formal monoid operation |
| Empty history | Candidate only in neutral layer | Not central | Explicit identity |
| Global closure | Not pre-formal | Explicit assumption | Formal monoid domain; completed domain still framework-specific |
| Freeness/universal property | Not pre-formal | No | Explicit assumption |

## Main result

The two formal matches converge on the same finite constructional behavior. Their disagreement concerns the **place and form of the additional formal commitment**.

Peano places the decisive burden on a distinguished initial element, successor, and global closure/induction. The free-monoid reading places the decisive burden on a formal monoid structure, identity, generator, and freeness.

Therefore:

> The neutral construction supplies a common finite structural core, while the route from that core to a complete and canonical mathematical object depends on the formal framework chosen.

## What this establishes

1. The same pre-formal evidence supports at least two independent formal interpretations.
2. The natural-number correspondence is stable across both matches at the level of finite generation, extension, and depth.
3. Framework-specific commitments must not be retroactively attributed to the neutral construction.
4. The remaining question is no longer whether the construction can be represented as a natural-number structure at all, but which formal commitments are required to make the representation complete, canonical, and mathematically legitimate.

## What remains open

- Whether the common finite core determines a unique abstract structure under a sufficiently weak formal specification.
- Whether Initial Algebra or Natural-Number Object interpretations collapse the remaining ambiguity or merely relocate it.
- Whether a framework-neutral statement of the target can be formulated strongly enough to support a uniqueness result.
- Whether the final result should identify \(\mathbb N\) itself or only a structure categorically/algebraically equivalent to it under an explicitly declared framework.

## Rule for Phase 9

No result proved using Peano axioms, monoid axioms, freeness, initiality, or categorical structure is promoted to a pre-formal fact.
