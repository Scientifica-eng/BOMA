# PHASE-10-001 — Forward/Reverse Integration

## Status

Stage 10 — integration of the forward construction and reverse-engineering paths.

## Purpose

Test whether the structure obtained by the forward construction path and the behavioral core obtained by reverse engineering describe the same constructional object, once the constitutive formalization boundary is made explicit.

## Source paths

The project roadmap defines the active mission as:

```text
pre-numerical foundation → natural numbers
```

and requires that constructional choices be distinguished from mathematically forced consequences. The provisional starting-point record begins from conditions of construction, minimal distinguishability, and a possible distinguished entity, explicitly without assuming a completed mathematical universe. citeturn120file0turn121file0

The reverse path has independently extracted a finite generative core consisting of an initial structural role, repeatable elementary extension, finite histories, decomposition, construction depth, and coherent finite composition behavior.

## Main integration result

The two paths converge on the same finite generative pattern:

```text
conditions for construction
        ↓
minimal distinction
        ↓
distinguished initial role
        ↓
repeatable elementary extension
        ↓
finite construction history
        ↓
construction depth
        ↓
composable / recoverable histories
        ↓
successor-chain behavior
```

This convergence is significant because the forward route began from a deliberately weak pre-numerical hypothesis, while the reverse route began from the completed target and decomposed it backward.

The strongest current claim is therefore:

> The forward and reverse investigations independently converge on a common finite generative successor-like core. This supports structural coherence of the project, but does not yet prove that the pre-formal starting point uniquely generates the full natural-number universe.

## What is integrated

The following can now be treated as a shared constructional interface between the two paths:

1. distinguishability sufficient for a distinguished role;
2. a repeatable elementary extension;
3. finite generation by repeated extension;
4. recoverability/decomposition of finite histories;
5. construction depth as a stage invariant;
6. sequential accumulation/composition.

## What remains explicitly external

The integration does **not** derive the following from the common pre-formal core:

- a formal total domain;
- a formal equality/identity relation;
- a global completion principle;
- induction as a logical axiom;
- monoid structure and freeness;
- initial-algebra initiality;
- categorical NNO structure;
- a framework-independent proof that the completed object is uniquely ℕ.

These remain constitutive/formalization commitments identified in Phase 9.

## Constitutive boundary

The integrated pipeline must therefore remain visibly two-layered:

```text
PRE-FORMAL / CONSTRUCTIONAL
────────────────────────────────
minimal distinction

              ↓

CONSTITUTIVE FORMALIZATION CHOICE
────────────────────────────────

              ↓

FORMAL NATURAL-NUMBER STRUCTURE
────────────────────────────────
0 / successor or equivalent representation
induction or equivalent closure principle
addition
multiplication

```

The constitutive boundary is not to be retroactively described as a theorem of the pre-formal layer.

## Decision

The forward and reverse paths are considered **integrated at the finite structural level**.

They are **not yet integrated at the level of full formal natural-number existence or uniqueness**.

This distinction is now a formal project invariant.

## Next test

The next integration experiment should test the first concrete formal realization regime selected by the project and verify, component by component, that:

1. the forward construction maps into the formal object;
2. the reverse-extracted invariants are preserved;
3. the mapping is faithful on construction depth and finite composition;
4. the additional formal commitments are exactly those previously declared and no hidden assumptions are introduced.
