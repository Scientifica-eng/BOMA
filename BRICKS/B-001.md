## 1. Identity

**Brick ID:** `B-001`

**Working name:** Minimal Inductive Object Domain

B-001 is the first experimental Brick constructed from the empty BOMA development `D-000`.

It is intentionally **not** identified with natural numbers, arithmetic, or any complete mathematical theory. Its purpose is to provide the smallest useful object-level construction with:

- a distinguished initial object;
- a successor constructor.

The architectural atomicity of this combination remains under investigation.

---

## 2. BOMA Content

At the current experimental level, B-001 proposes an object domain containing:

```text
BOMAObject
├── initial
└── successor : BOMAObject → BOMAObject
```

No addition, multiplication, ordering, induction theorem, or arithmetic law is part of B-001.

### Current mathematical reading

B-001 introduces a domain of objects together with an initial object and a unary successor-forming operation. The exact semantic status of this structure is deliberately left minimal; no stronger interpretation is assumed merely because the Lean representation resembles a familiar inductive construction.

## 8. Atomicity

**Status:** `OPEN`

The current candidate is:

```text
B-001
├── initial
└── successor
```

A possible decomposition under investigation is:

```text
B-001a: minimal pointed object domain
B-001b: successor structure
```

This decomposition is **not adopted** merely because the backend declaration can be syntactically split. The question is whether the proposed parts have independent BOMA meaning and whether separating them improves the architectural model.
